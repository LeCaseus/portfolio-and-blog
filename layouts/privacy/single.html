DEV NOTE // This is a personal, non-commercial site run by moi. Hence, this page just explains what gets collected when you visit and what you can do about it.
CH-12.1WHAT I KEEP

analytics. self-hosted umami. it records page URL, referrer, browser type, and an approximate country from your IP. no cookies, no persistent visitor ID, no cross-site tracking. the script doesn't even load until you interact with the page — move a mouse, scroll, tap, press a key — so bots and instant bounces aren't recorded.

reactions (the ♥ on posts). your IP is hashed with SHA-256 on receipt and only the hash is stored, tied to the post slug, to stop duplicate reactions. the hash is one-way but not unguessable — it's not stored alongside your name or anything else that identifies you.

guestbook and post comments. name and website are optional. message is required. name, website and message appear publicly once i approve the entry. your IP is hashed the same way as above, to enforce a limit of five submissions per hour across the guestbook and all comments combined. nothing goes live until i've read it.

incoming webmentions. if another page on the web links here and sends a webmention, my server fetches that page to verify the link is real, then stores the source URL, the kind of mention (reply, like, repost, mention), whatever the page publicly marks up about its author (name, profile URL), and up to 280 characters of its text. nothing appears on the site until i approve it. IP is hashed, ten per hour limit.

theme preference. stored only in your browser's localStorage. never sent to me.

server and security logs. cloudflare and crowdsec see your IP and standard request metadata for routing and abuse prevention. their retention is their own business — see their policies.

none of this is used for ads, profiling, or automated decisions.

CH-12.2HOW LONG

everything i store, i keep indefinitely — analytics, reaction hashes, approved guestbook entries and comments, approved webmentions. there's no auto-expiry. the reason is that traffic stats are only useful if they're continuous, and guestbook entries are records of the site's history.

pending entries stay until i approve or delete them. rejected messages are deleted immediately. rejected webmentions are kept as rejected so i can spot patterns, until i clear them out by hand.

CH-12.3THIRD PARTIES

nothing is sold or shared for anyone's commercial purposes. ever.

cloudflare sits in front of the origin as CDN, DNS, and reverse proxy. your request may route through servers outside your country. see their privacy policy.

crowdsec runs alongside the server for intrusion prevention. self-hosted, so the data stays on my hardware.

webring embeds on the home page load scripts directly from the ring's own servers (softmachine.dev, baccyflap.com, literring.neocities.org, cdn.jsdelivr.net). i have no control over what those set or log. check their policies if you're curious.

analytics, reactions, guestbook, comments and webmentions all run on hardware i own. that data doesn't leave my infrastructure.

CH-12.4YOUR RIGHTS

if GDPR or New Zealand's Privacy Act applies to you, you can ask me to show you what i have, correct something wrong, delete something, restrict how it's used, or give you a portable copy. you can also object to processing and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority if you think i've handled something badly.

in practice: IP hashes are pseudonymous rather than anonymous — a determined effort could reverse them — so they count as personal data here, but there's no stored link to a name. a deletion request for a hash usually just confirms nothing else identifiable exists. for a guestbook entry, comment or webmention, tell me which one is yours (the page it was posted on and the name you used, or just the message text if you posted anonymously) and i'll delete it.

email me at records [at] cheztervargas [dot] com.

CH-12.5COOKIES

no cookies. the only client-side storage is localStorage for theme preference and sessionStorage for the blog sidebar state, which clears when you close the tab. neither reaches me. webring embeds may set their own cookies outside my control.
